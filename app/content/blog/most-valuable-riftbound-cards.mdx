Riftbound — the League of Legends Trading Card Game from Riot Games — is new, which
means its secondary market is still finding its feet. That's exactly when it pays to
watch prices closely. Here's what drives value in Riftbound and how to track it.

## What makes a Riftbound card valuable

The same forces that price any trading card apply, with a launch-era twist:

- **Rarity** — higher-rarity and foil printings carry the biggest premiums. Browse a
  set by rarity to find its chase cards.
- **Popular champions** — cards tied to fan-favorite League champions (think Ahri,
  Kai'Sa, Draven, Irelia, Vex) tend to draw the most demand.
- **Alternate arts and special treatments** — extended-art and premium versions are the
  cards collectors chase hardest.
- **First-set scarcity** — early Origins pulls have the nostalgia-of-the-launch factor
  that often ages well for a game's debut set.

## A note on honesty

Because Riftbound is brand new, prices are more volatile than an established game like
Pokémon, and thin sales history can make figures jumpy. Treat early prices as a moving
snapshot, not a settled value — and always check the live number before you buy or sell.
