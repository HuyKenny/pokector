Riftbound, the League of Legends Trading Card Game by Riot Games, launched on
**31 October 2025** and has expanded quickly. Here's every set in release order, so you
know what's out and where each card fits.

## The sets, in order

- **Origins** — released **31 October 2025**. The debut set that launched the game and
  established its core cards. [Browse Origins →](/riftbound/sets/origins)
- **Origins: Proving Grounds** — the Origins-era organized-play / entry expansion.
  [Browse Proving Grounds →](/riftbound/sets/origins-proving-grounds)
- **Spiritforged** — released **13 February 2026**. The first major expansion after
  launch. [Browse Spiritforged →](/riftbound/sets/spiritforged)
- **Unleashed** — released **8 May 2026**. [Browse Unleashed →](/riftbound/sets/unleashed)
- **Vendetta** — released **31 July 2026**, the newest expansion.
  [Browse Vendetta →](/riftbound/sets/vendetta)

Alongside these, Riftbound has promotional and organized-play cards handed out at events
and in special products.

## How the sets differ

Each set adds new champions, cards and chase rarities. As with any TCG, the **debut set
(Origins)** carries launch-era significance, while newer expansions like **Unleashed**
and **Vendetta** bring the freshest cards and the current chase alt-arts. Higher-rarity
and foil printings are where the value concentrates in every set.
